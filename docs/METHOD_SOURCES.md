# Method sources

This page lists the methods literature behind each of the 14 AMICUS method checklists: iv, did, rdd, conjoint, survey_exp, field_exp, text, matching, mediation, multilevel, panel, synth, measurement, and hte. Each checklist was generated only from the sources listed for its module. Each check cites the pages that support it. The list has citations and DOIs only: no full text, no excerpts, and no PDFs. Follow the DOI to read a paper at its publisher. The universal checklist has its own source list, under "Sources" in `UNIVERSAL_CHECKLIST.md`.

For 12 of the 14 modules, fixed rules chose the sources. The candidate pool comes from what current research cites and teaches. It has research articles published in 2010, 2015, 2020, and 2025 in eight political science and economics journals (APSR, AJPS, JOP, AER, Econometrica, JPE, QJE, REStud). It also has the reading lists of graduate methods courses in economics and political science departments. A work enters a module by one of three recorded rules. The anchor rule: at least two of those articles that use the module's method cite the work, and the work is methods-oriented. The syllabus rule: the work is on at least two graduate syllabi and is methods-oriented. The methods-journal fallback applies only when the first two rules leave a module with fewer than ten sources. It ranks articles in ten methods journals whose abstracts name the method by age-adjusted citation count.

The syllabus rule is only partly in use. We have not yet sorted by method most works that are on two or more syllabi. Most works that would qualify only through teaching, textbook chapters in particular, are therefore not in these lists.

For `measurement` and `hte`, the rules did not find enough sources. A language model proposed the sources for these two modules, and we approved them. The rules above did not admit these sources, and their records carry no admission rule. Each entry below shows how the work was admitted. The full account is in [SKILL_GENERATION.md](SKILL_GENERATION.md).

Admission and use are separate. Every admitted source entered generation. A source supports a check only when a claim from it survived into a shipped check. Each module section therefore has two parts: the sources that one or more shipped checks cite, with the number of checks that cite each, and the sources that were admitted but are cited by no shipped check. A work held as more than one document in the corpus is listed once. A check that cites several documents of the same work counts once.
