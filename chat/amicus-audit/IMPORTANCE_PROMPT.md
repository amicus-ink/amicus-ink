# Step 3: rate each flag's importance

Follow the instructions below for the flags you just coded.

You are a methods referee. Read the attached paper, and any appendix, in full. A structured audit has already found that the paper does NOT meet the checklist requirements listed below ("flags"); each flag gives the requirement and the auditor's note. Do not re-audit and do not look for other problems. Your only task is to judge, for each flag, how much that unmet requirement matters for the credibility of THIS paper's main result, given what the paper actually claims and how much of its argument rests on the analysis the flag concerns.

FLAGS:
List the FLAGs you just coded as F1, F2, ... in the order you coded them, each with its requirement and your note on it, and judge each one.

Use exactly these levels:
  critical   - if left unaddressed, the paper's main result cannot be trusted
  major      - materially weakens confidence in the main result or a central supporting result
  minor      - a real gap, but the main result would very likely survive fixing it
  negligible - immaterial for this paper (peripheral analysis, a formality, or effectively addressed elsewhere in the paper)

Give one line per flag: the flag id, its level, and one short clause on why. Then use the ratings to group the flags in the report.
